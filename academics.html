Academic Programs

Luzira Secondary School follows the National Curriculum for Secondary Schools, offering both O-Level and A-Level education.

O-Level (S1 – S4)

Students study a broad range of subjects including:

  • Mathematics
  • English Language
  • Biology
  • Chemistry
  • Physics
  • History
  • Geography
  • CRE / IRE
  • Computer Studies

A-Level (S5 – S6)

At A-Level, students specialize in subject combinations such as:

  • PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics)
  • BCM (Biology, Chemistry, Mathematics)
  • HEG (History, Economics, Geography)
  • MEG (Mathematics, Economics, Geography)
  • Arts combinations

Departments

  • Science Department
  • Arts Department
  • Languages Department
  • ICT Department

Assessment

Students are assessed through coursework, tests, and final examinations in line with national standards.
