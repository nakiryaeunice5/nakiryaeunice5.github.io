Admissions

We welcome students who are committed to academic excellence and good discipline. Admissions are open for both O-Level and A-Level.

Admission Requirements

  • Completed application form
  • Previous academic reports
  • Birth certificate
  • Passport photographs

Application Process

  1. Obtain and fill in the application form
  2. Submit required documents
  3. Attend interview (if required)
  4. Receive admission decision
