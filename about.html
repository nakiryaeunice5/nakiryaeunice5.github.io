About Our School

Luzira Secondary School was established to provide quality education in accordance with the national curriculum. We are committed to academic excellence, discipline, and holistic student development.

Our Mission

To nurture responsible, knowledgeable, and skilled students who can contribute positively to society.

Our Vision

To be a leading institution in providing quality education and producing competent graduates.

Core Values

  • Discipline
  • Integrity
  • Excellence
  • Respect
  • Hard Work

Our History

Since its establishment in 1984 under the Luzira Archdeaconry, Namirembe Diocese, the school has grown in both academic performance and infrastructure, becoming one of the most respected schools in the region. It has been of huge impact to Luzira community and Uganda at large.
